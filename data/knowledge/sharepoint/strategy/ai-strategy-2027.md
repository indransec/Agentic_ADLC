# AI strategy 2027

## Goals

Use AI agents to remove manual triage and document checks across claims, service and lending,
while a person keeps every decision that affects a customer's money or eligibility.

## Priorities

1. Claims triage automation (travel first), measured by handling time and re-work.
2. Internal service productivity (IT and HR service desks).
3. Lending pre-screening, only with model risk approval and explainable outputs.

## Principles

Agents are built through the agent development lifecycle; every agent has an accountable business
owner, approved data access and a kill switch.
