# AI use policy

## Scope

Applies to every AI agent or model that reads Halcyon data or interacts with customers or staff.

## Risk tiers

- **High**: the agent informs or takes decisions on credit, insurance eligibility, pricing,
  employment or payouts above 1,000 EUR; or reads restricted data.
- **Medium**: customer-facing drafts or recommendations reviewed by staff; personal data that is
  masked or minimised; payouts up to 1,000 EUR approved by a person.
- **Low**: internal productivity with internal data and no customer impact.

## Human oversight

A person approves every action that moves money, changes a contract or declines a customer.
Agents may act alone only on reversible internal actions.

## Kill switch

Every agent in production has a kill switch owned by its service owner.
