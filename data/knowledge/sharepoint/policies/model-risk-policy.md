# Model risk policy

## Applicability

Models and agents that inform credit or insurance decisions are high risk and need model risk
committee approval before production.

## Requirements

Explainability for every adverse outcome; a challenger evaluation; fairness testing across
protected groups with a maximum gap of 5 percentage points; annual revalidation.
