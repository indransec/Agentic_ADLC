# Privacy policy for AI processing

## Minimisation

Personal data is masked before it reaches a model unless the use case's data strategy approves it.
Customer identifiers are pseudonymised; free-text fields are scanned for personal data.

## Retention

Conversation memory holding personal data is kept at most 30 days.
