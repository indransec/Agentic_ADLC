# SOP: claims triage

## Fast-track rules

A travel claim is fast-track when the policy is active, the claim type is covered, every required
document is present and the claimed amount is under 300 EUR.

## Escalation

Claims with suspected fraud indicators, medical claims and claims over 1,000 EUR always go to an
assessor. Customers who ask for a person are transferred within one working day.
