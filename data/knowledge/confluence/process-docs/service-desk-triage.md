# Process: service desk ticket triage

## Steps

1. Read the ticket and attachments.
2. Set category (access, hardware, software, incident) and priority P1 to P4.
3. Route to the resolver group; P1 incidents also page the on-call engineer.
4. Reply to the requester with the expected resolution time.

## Service levels

P1 assigned within 15 minutes, P2 within 1 hour, P3 and P4 within 4 hours.
