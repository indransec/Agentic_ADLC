# Process: loan application

## Steps

1. Application received with identity, income and employment details.
2. Pre-screening: completeness, affordability indicators, policy exclusions.
3. Underwriting by a credit analyst, with the credit bureau report.
4. Decision by a credit officer; declines are explained in writing.
