# Process: travel claims handling

## Steps

1. Receive the claim (web form or email) with receipts and trip details.
2. Check the policy is active for the trip dates and covers the claim type.
3. Check the documents: booking confirmation, receipts, carrier delay letter for delays.
4. Classify: fast track (complete, under 300 EUR) or assessment.
5. Ask the customer for missing documents (one consolidated request).
6. Settle: a handler approves fast-track payouts; an assessor approves the rest.

## Service levels

Acknowledge within 1 hour; triage within 1 working day; fast-track payout within 3 days.
