# Capability: Consumer lending

Halcyon Bank offers personal loans from 1,000 to 50,000 EUR. About 3,200 applications arrive each
month through the web and branch channels.

## Pre-screening

Before full underwriting, analysts pre-screen applications for completeness, affordability
indicators and policy exclusions. Pre-screening takes 25 minutes per application. Credit decisions
are regulated: every decline must be explainable to the applicant and reviewed by a credit officer.

## Ownership

The head of consumer lending is the business owner; the chief credit risk officer is accountable
for credit decisions.
