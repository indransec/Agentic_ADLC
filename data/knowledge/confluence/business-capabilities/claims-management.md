# Capability: Claims management

Halcyon Group's claims management capability covers first notice of loss, triage, assessment,
settlement and recovery for travel, home and motor insurance.

## Travel claims

About 9,500 travel claims a month (trip delay, cancellation, baggage, medical). Handlers spend
twelve minutes on average triaging a claim before an assessor sees it; 40% of claims come back for
missing receipts. Claims operations owns the process; the head of claims operations is the
business owner of claims automation.

## Triage

Triage checks policy cover, trip dates, the claim type and the documents supplied, then routes the
claim to fast-track settlement (under 300 EUR, complete documents) or to an assessor.

## Settlement

Payouts are always approved by a person. Fast-track payouts up to 300 EUR are approved by a claims
handler; larger payouts by an assessor.
