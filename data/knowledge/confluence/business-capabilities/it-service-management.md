# Capability: IT service management

The IT service desk handles about 14,000 tickets a month from 6,000 employees: access requests,
password resets, hardware faults, software requests and incidents.

## Triage and routing

First-line analysts read each ticket, set a category and priority and route it to one of 18
resolver groups. Mis-routing causes 22% of tickets to be reassigned at least once; the median time
to first assignment is 35 minutes.

## Ownership

The service desk manager owns the triage process; the IT operations director is accountable.
